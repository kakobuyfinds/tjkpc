Check sizing and QC before shipping

Match the listing to your measurements

For hoodies, tees and jackets, measure the chest width and length of a garment that fits you. Compare those measurements with the seller’s chart using the same units and measurement method. For slides, check how the seller defines foot or insole length.

Review photos of your exact item

Compare the warehouse photos with your selected color and size. For shoes, check the pair, labels and soles. For clothing, check seams, closures and visible marks. Request a ruler photo if a measurement would change your decision.

Compare shipping quotes on equal terms

Use the same destination, packed weight and parcel dimensions when comparing routes. Check which quote includes insurance or taxes, and verify the seller’s return window before approving dispatch.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product directory for finding and comparing listings you may want to order through a shopping agent. This page pairs selected CAFANS listings with a checklist for sizing, warehouse photos and shipping.

How do I order a product I find here?

Open the CAFANS product listing, check the style and size, and review the available shopping-agent options. If CSSBuy is available for the listing, verify the selected variant and fees on the order page before paying.

What do the catalog prices cover?

The catalog shows estimated item prices in USD. Check the order page for domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees and any applicable taxes.
